Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about using Excuva responsibly.

Can I edit and rewrite the generated response?

Yes! You can edit any output directly in the live editor, or use 1-click transformation pills (Make Shorter, More Formal, More Casual, More Apologetic) or custom AI prompts.

Are my explanations stored securely?

All generated records and documents are stored securely in your encrypted Neon PostgreSQL database. You maintain complete control to delete individual records or wipe your entire history at any time.

How does the Supporting Document generator work?

The document generator produces 8 formal declaration templates (such as Personal Declarations, Extension Requests, and Leave Notices) that can be edited in a live WYSIWYG sheet and downloaded as a formatted PDF.
